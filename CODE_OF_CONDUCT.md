# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the NGI541 project a respectful,
professional, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We expect contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and other participants to act
in ways that support a healthy technical community.

## Our standards

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- treating other participants with respect;
- discussing technical disagreements constructively;
- giving and accepting technical feedback professionally;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- focusing criticism on code, designs, evidence, and technical decisions rather
  than individuals;
- respecting confidentiality when information has been shared privately.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct;
- insulting, derogatory, or deliberately disruptive comments;
- personal or political attacks directed at project participants;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting confidential, proprietary, or unlawfully obtained
  material;
- behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  engineering environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all NGI541 project spaces, including:

- repositories;
- issues and pull requests;
- code review;
- discussions;
- project communication channels;
- project events;
- situations where a participant is officially representing NGI541.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these
standards.

They may remove, edit, reject, or restrict contributions or participation that
violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on severity and recurrence, responses may include:

1. a private correction;
2. a formal warning;
3. a temporary restriction from project participation;
4. a permanent restriction from project participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate,
and applied consistently.

## Reporting

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to:

**contact@ngi541.org**

Use the subject prefix:

`[CODE OF CONDUCT]`

Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality. Project maintainers
will avoid unnecessary disclosure of the reporter's identity or other private
information.
